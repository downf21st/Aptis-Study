      - strong: PART 3 TEMPLATE
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE 1: Opinion"
    - list:
      - listitem: In my opinion, + opinion.
      - listitem: Moreover, + reason.
      - listitem: As a result, + positive/negative effect.
    - paragraph: "Example: In my opinion, technology has made communication much easier. Moreover, people can connect with others around the world instantly. As a result, building relationships has become more convenient than before."
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE 2: Experience"
    - list:
      - listitem: I usually/often + activity.
      - listitem: Additionally/Sometimes, + second activity.
      - listitem: Overall, + benefit/feeling.
    - paragraph: "Example: I usually read books before going to sleep. Additionally, I sometimes visit libraries at weekends to find new novels. Overall, reading helps me relax and expand my knowledge."
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE 3: Advice"
    - list:
      - listitem: I think + suggestion.
      - listitem: It will help somebody + benefit.
      - listitem: Moreover, + second benefit.
    - paragraph: "Example: I think taking a course would be a great idea. It will help you improve your skills more effectively. Moreover, you can receive guidance from experienced teachers."

      - strong: FORMAL EMAIL TEMPLATE
    - paragraph: Dear Sir/Madam,
    - paragraph: I am writing in response to your email regarding + topic. I was very excited/disappointed to hear this news because + reason.
    - paragraph: In my opinion, + main opinion/problem. Moreover, + explanation/example. I believe that this issue may affect members in several ways.
    - paragraph: To resolve this issue, I would recommend that the club should + suggestion. This would help members + benefit. In addition, + second benefit/result.
    - paragraph: I strongly believe that these changes would improve the overall experience for members and make the club more appealing.
    - paragraph: I look forward to hearing your response.
    - paragraph: Yours sincerely,
    - paragraph: Mary
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE: Suggesting a Place"
    - list:
      - listitem: In terms of location, I would suggest + place because + reason.
      - listitem: It is + adjective + and + adjective.
      - listitem: Moreover, + additional benefit.
    - paragraph: "Example: In terms of location, I would suggest organizing the event at a coffee shop in the city centre because it is convenient and easily accessible. Moreover, the atmosphere is comfortable and suitable for members of different ages."
    - paragraph: "Convenient: easily accessible, located in the city centre, convenient for members"
    - paragraph: "Comfortable: relaxing atmosphere, comfortable environment"
    - paragraph: "Suitable: suitable for all age groups, ideal venue"
    - paragraph: "Transportation: easy to travel to, close to public transport"
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE: Suggesting a Guest Speaker/Celebrity"
    - list:
      - listitem: I would recommend inviting + person/job title because + reason.
      - listitem: Not only is he/she + quality, but he/she also + second quality.
      - listitem: I believe that his/her appearance would + result.
    - paragraph: "Example: I would recommend inviting a well-known entrepreneur because not only is he highly experienced, but he is also an inspiring public speaker. I believe that his appearance would attract more participants and motivate young people."
    - paragraph: "Influencer: has a strong influence on young people"
    - paragraph: "Chef: has extensive cooking experience"
    - paragraph: "Artist: is well known for creativity"
    - paragraph: "Businessperson: has practical business knowledge"
    - paragraph: "Athlete: inspires people to live healthily"
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "TEMPLATE: Giving Advice/Suggestions"
    - list:
      - listitem: To address this issue, I would recommend that + subject + should + verb.
      - listitem: This would help + somebody + benefit.
      - listitem: Moreover, + second benefit.
    - paragraph: "Example: To address this issue, I would recommend that the club should organize more outdoor activities. This would help members relax and connect with one another more easily. Moreover, it could attract more young participants."
    - list:
      - listitem: In my opinion, the best solution would be to + verb.
      - listitem: By doing this, + positive result.
    - paragraph: "Example: In my opinion, the best solution would be to create official accounts for all members. By doing this, the club could reduce negative comments and improve communication between members."
    - list:
      - listitem: I think it would be a good idea to + verb because + reason.
    - paragraph: "Example: I think it would be a good idea to separate contestants into different age groups because it would make the competition fairer."
